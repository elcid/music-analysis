# Beethoven, Große Fuge op. 133 — fugue analysis with playable examples

Published site: https://elcid.github.io/music-analysis/

A self-contained analysis of the fugue structure of Beethoven's Große Fuge, op. 133,
with eight engraved examples, synthesised audio and downloadable MIDI for every subject
entry. Open `index.html`; no server or dependencies are needed.

Sections: the subject and its countersubject; the Overtura's four statements; the first
fugue's exposition and three variations; the "Meno mosso"; the scherzo; the augmented
subject of the last fugue; the form as a whole and the readings in the literature.

Sources and method are stated in section 9 of the page. The score is the Eulenburg
miniature score (E. E. 1198, No. 93); bar numbers are the printed ones.
